This chapter collects the mathematics the later chapters use, in the notation used there, with short proofs and a worked numerical example for each topic. It covers four bodies of mathematics: genotype frequencies, Hardy Weinberg proportions and linkage disequilibrium; average effects as a weighted least squares projection and the additive genetic variance; the Wright Fisher and Moran models, Kimura's diffusion and fixation probabilities; and the theory of exchangeable random partitions, with de Finetti's theorem, the Polya urn, the Dirichlet distribution, Kingman's paintbox, the Ewens sampling formula and the site frequency spectrum. It closes with the Bayesian and likelihood free devices of the book: the horseshoe shrinkage weight, rejection approximate Bayesian computation, the check loss that identifies quantiles, and noise outsourcing. The statements are standard and are not claimed as new. A reader who knows this material can start at the chapter that interests them and return here for notation.

## From the literature

Hardy Weinberg and the decay of disequilibrium are classical (Hardy 1908, Weinberg 1908). Kimura's fixation formula is due to Kimura. De Finetti's theorem is in the form of Hewitt and Savage. Kingman's paintbox is Kingman (1978). The Ewens sampling formula is Ewens (1972), and Watterson's results on segregating sites are Watterson (1975).
